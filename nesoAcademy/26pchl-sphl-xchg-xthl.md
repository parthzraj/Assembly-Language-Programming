## 8085 Instruction Notes

13) **PCHL** — PC ← HL  
    Loads the contents of the HL register pair into the Program Counter.

14) **SPHL** — SP ← HL  
    Loads the contents of the HL register pair into the Stack Pointer.

15) **XCHG** — HL ↔ DE  
    Exchanges the contents of the HL register pair with the DE register pair.

16) **XTHL** — [SP] ↔ HL  
    Exchanges the contents of HL with the top of the stack (the data pointed to by SP).
